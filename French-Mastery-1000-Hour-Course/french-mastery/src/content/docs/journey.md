---
title: Your 1,000-hour journey
description: The complete timetable with 100 guided ten-hour units.
---


Ten stages × ten units × ten hours = **1,000 hours**. Units include lesson study, independent input, vocabulary, use, and review. Exact CEFR progress varies by learner. At ten hours weekly this is about 100 weeks; at fifteen, about 67; at twenty, 50.
