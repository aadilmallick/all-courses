## What a reference cannot replace

Knowing a conjugation table is different from selecting the form while listening or speaking. Each chapter connects forms to decisions about meaning, then asks you to use them. Revisit a family in reading, translation, listening, and interaction. A formal literary tense may require recognition more urgently than spontaneous production; a common object pronoun needs repeated active use.
