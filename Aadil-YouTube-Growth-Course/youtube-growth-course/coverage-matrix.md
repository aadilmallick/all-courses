# Outcome-to-evidence map

| Requested ability | Lessons | Worked instructional material | Actual companion or artifact | Verification boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choose at least three fitting channel ideas | 01–02 | Three complete positioning concepts, a fourth experiment, proof and demand distinctions | Niche scorecard;72 proposed premises | Profile evidence inspected; demand remains a hypothesis |
| Learn from Fireship and peers | 03 | Six creator studies with observation, inference and adaptation | Three packaging briefs; promise worksheet | Public pages/descriptions/indexes, not full playback or private analytics |
| Script and teach well | 04 | Full original technical script, intermediate states, shot plan and claim ledger | Script timer and production templates | Original instructional example, not a filmed result |
| Film readable technical videos | 05 | Screen/voice/occasional-camera setup, audio diagnosis, container and track tests | Storage/setup/retake model | Official documentation researched; hardware/editor untested |
| Learn editing and current AI production tools | 06 | Four-pass edit, specific first-edit lab, captions, sound, delivery, measured AI trial | Manual-versus-AI time experiment and editor training links | Capabilities researched; no vendor output benchmark performed |
| Grow through publishing and feedback | 02,03,07 | Audience research, titles/thumbnails, traffic-mix math and retention diagnosis | Idea-ranking, packaging and analytics labs | Synthetic cohorts; no promised algorithm or universal benchmarks |
| Build marketing funnels | 08 | Lead magnet, actual seven-email example, notes/tutoring/service/community boundaries | Funnel calculator and business templates | Draft learning materials; no mailing or deployment |
| Plan for$10k monthly revenue | 09 | Exact diversified gross model, costs, downside case, conversion denominators and workload | Economics calculator | Hypothetical inputs, before tax/owner labor; exceeds starting capacity |
| Act every day sustainably | 10 | Six-video season, time boxes, recovery and longer-term evidence gates | Ninety daily actions and capacity calculator | Process targets, no audience or income deadline |

Prerequisite order: choose a viewer → research a problem → package the promise → script the explanation → capture evidence → edit → publish and review → validate the next useful offer → model economics → repeat within a real calendar. The economics and production experiments implement the numerical relationships rather than showing prerecorded results.
