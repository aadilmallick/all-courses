# Your 90-day daily YouTube playbook

Start with **Day 1 on a Monday** and use the day numbers as the portable schedule. An optional dated example is Monday, November 16, 2026 through Saturday, February 13, 2027. Read the matching lesson section when the task requires it; reading, setup, and the brief time log belong inside each listed block.

The baseline is **8.5 hours per week**: Monday 60 minutes, Tuesday 75, Wednesday 60, Thursday 75, Friday 30, Saturday 150, Sunday 60. Each fourteen-day cycle is seventeen hours. Six cycles use 102 hours, followed by 7.5 hours of consolidation across days 85–90: **109.5 hours total**.

At eight hours weekly, reduce scope or cadence because this baseline is half an hour over. At ten hours, retain the additional 1.5 hours as buffer until measured time makes room for more. Optional Shorts, learning, outreach, and customer delivery use the same capacity. Do not add them invisibly on top of it.

Six core long videos have planned release days **14, 28, 42, 56, 70, and 84**. Release only when the actual quality gate passes. If an important claim, sound issue, or broken export remains, record the blocker and revise the plan. Eight videos are a stretch only after evidence shows they fit. No audience or earnings result is promised, and the $10,000 scenario is not a day-90 expectation.

These are learner actions for future use. Creating this schedule did not publish videos, contact people, collect addresses, create accounts, buy software, or deploy a funnel. Where a task mentions outside feedback, use appropriate public or permitted sources; if no suitable person is available, record the missing evidence rather than inventing it.

After every block record **actual minutes, concrete output, and the next action**. Stop when the time box ends, simplify when needed, and use the next review to decide what changes. The structured version is [90-day-actions.json](90-day-actions.json).

## Budget and evidence checks

The rows contain ninety distinct actions, not ninety uploads. The first eighty-four days form six complete seventeen-hour cycles. Days 85–90 consolidate the library, audience evidence, business observations, and next season. Only actual results belong in the review; unknown conversion rates stay unknown.

The plan follows [Chapter 10](../chapters/10-daily-playbook-and-capstone.md). See [Chapter 9](../chapters/09-economics-and-sustainable-income.md) when paid work competes with production time, and recheck the announced February 2027 platform changes when eligibility becomes relevant.
