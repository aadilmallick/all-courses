# Authoring brief

**Reader:** a programmer who wants mathematical and implementation mastery of language models and related systems. Prior transformer, probability, calculus, reinforcement-learning, browser-API, and GPU expertise is not assumed. Basic Python execution is introduced in the preface; necessary mathematical prerequisites appear before use.

**Target abilities:** trace tensor indices; derive attention and policy gradients; reconstruct a causal decoder; implement and test supervised and preference losses; distinguish model, objective, data, execution, retrieval, orchestration, and evaluation; identify where a demonstration does not establish a larger claim.

**Input:** the previously authored 54-lesson curriculum archive. Its content hash and review scope are in sources.json. The external primer and primary-paper ledger is inherited with its original access limits.

**Scope:** 18 main chapters in dependency order, four reference appendices, a continuous book, complete Python examples, and 18 small browser experiments. Sparse/distributed kernels, large-model training, actual judge services, trained visual encoders, and hardware control are explanatory extensions rather than claimed executed systems.

**Conventions:** row-vector features; batch-token-feature arrays; natural logarithms; masks with True meaning allowed; explicit loss reductions; separate old/current/reference policies; seeded or deterministic fixtures. Numerical demonstrations use ordinary CPU floating point.

**Companion:** dependency-free browser modules and a local Node server; no hosted site or external account. Canonical calculations are in companion/engine.js. Python examples are canonical runnable source. Teaching text is canonical in chapters/ and preface.md; tools/build_book.py assembles the continuous volume and generated HTML/static output.

**Depth review:** main chapters have a motivating problem, relevant prerequisite definitions, numerical intermediate reasoning, code connected to those equations, diagnosed failure cases, companion links, and a transition. The structural word diagnostic excludes fenced code. A second narrative-only count in verification.json also excludes tables, headings, and equation notation; neither count certifies pedagogy by length alone.
