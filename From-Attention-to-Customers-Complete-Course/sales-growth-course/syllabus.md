# The 1,000-hour syllabus

Each module is a fully authored chapter. Read in order. The hundred ten-hour sessions are active-work blocks and may span multiple days. Field work may be private simulation, but its evidence must be labeled. No public posting, account connection or ad spend is required to study.

| 2. Customer research, positioning, and offers | 14 | 16 | 28 | 24 | 8 | 90 |
| 3. Content strategy, copywriting, and creative | 14 | 16 | 36 | 16 | 8 | 90 |
| 4. Prospecting, discovery, proposals, and sales | 16 | 20 | 36 | 36 | 12 | 120 |
| 5. LinkedIn authority and demand generation | 12 | 14 | 42 | 22 | 10 | 100 |

| 7. YouTube long-form, Shorts, and production | 18 | 22 | 64 | 24 | 12 | 140 |
| 8. Websites, email, digital products, and launches | 14 | 14 | 34 | 18 | 10 | 90 |
| 9. Measurement, experimentation, and paid acquisition | 14 | 16 | 38 | 22 | 10 | 100 |
| 10. Retention, operations, partnerships, and capstone | 16 | 18 | 48 | 26 | 12 | 120 |
| **Total** | 140 | 160 | 380 | 222 | 98 | **1,000** |

At 10 active hours/week: 100 weeks. At 20: 50 weeks. At 30: about 33⅓ weeks. At 40: 25 weeks. These are workload calculations, not promises of market results.
