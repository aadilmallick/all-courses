## Results

Record counts, rates, missing values, uncertainty, audience mix, simultaneous changes, cost and contribution. State what the design can establish and what it cannot. An inconclusive result is valid. Do not infer a formal two-sample verdict from Wilson-interval overlap. See Chapter 9.
