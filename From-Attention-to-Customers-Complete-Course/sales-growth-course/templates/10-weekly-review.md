# Weekly operating review — working template

Record the period and evidence state. Keep missing data separate from zero.

- What useful work was completed?
- What did suitable customers or learners actually do?
- What was collected, refunded and spent?
- What hours went to delivery, content, sales, research and administration?
- What failed or exceeded capacity?
- What is the strongest supported explanation?
- What remains uncertain?
- What will continue, change and stop?

Choose one next decision and the evidence needed. Keep channel jobs distinct; a search lesson and a service invitation need different interpretation. See Chapters 9–10.
