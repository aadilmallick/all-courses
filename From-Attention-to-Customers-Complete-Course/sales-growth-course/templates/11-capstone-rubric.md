# Capstone rubric

Declare service, education or tutoring track. Label business, data, customers, transactions, tests and results as real, private, synthetic or mixed with exact boundaries.

Score 0–4 for each dimension: buyer understanding, offer fit, evidence quality, content usefulness, platform adaptation, sales clarity, route usability, delivery readiness, measurement reasoning and operational sustainability.

0 absent/misleading; 1 weak; 2 usable with material gaps; 3 coherent/supported; 4 strong execution with explicit limits and verification.

Suggested completion: 32/40, no dimension below 2, no fabricated evidence or unresolved critical route failure. This is an instructional rubric, not accreditation. Attach reviewer observations, repairs and a next-decision memo. See Chapter 10.
