# Discovery guide — working template

Confirm purpose, duration, agenda and permission for any recording. Understand before recommending.

1. What happened in the most recent episode?
2. What has already been tried and observed?
3. What consequence matters, and what is estimated rather than known?
4. What would a useful result look like?
5. Who uses, approves, implements and pays?
6. What access, timing, budget and responsibility constraints exist?
7. What alternatives are reasonable?

Summarize the problem and ask for correction. Record fit, non-fit, missing evidence, and the mutually agreed next step. Do not turn a polite response into a commitment. See Chapter 4.
