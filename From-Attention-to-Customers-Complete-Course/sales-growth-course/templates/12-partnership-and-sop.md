# Partnership and procedure briefs

## Partnership

Record shared audience problem, complementary contribution, complete artifact/event, owners, timeline, rights, commercial terms, disclosure, permission for follow-up, and review criteria. A request for promotion alone is not a complete audience benefit.

## Operating procedure

Record purpose, trigger, owner, inputs, permitted actions, steps, output, quality check, failure response and revision date. Start with publishing, onboarding, checkout testing or delivery. Separate draft preparation from posting, messaging, charging or other consequential account actions.

Evaluate actual saved time, review burden and quality. Keep sensitive information and unnecessary access out of delegated work. See Chapter 10.
