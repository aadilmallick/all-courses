## How to use the references

Judson is an additional route through groups, rings, fields and applications. Stein develops arithmetic with computational examples and connections beyond the elementary syllabus. Milne is a more concise and demanding next step after the field-extension and Galois chapters; it is not the assumed starting level. The MIT pages help compare topic families with established undergraduate courses. They do not set the exact pacing, prerequisites, or credit status of this self-study book.

The course links the sources without importing their licenses into the new prose or redistributing their bytes. The source ledger is `sources.json`. Additional author checks, if performed while writing particular proofs, are recorded in the relevant chapter or verification report.
