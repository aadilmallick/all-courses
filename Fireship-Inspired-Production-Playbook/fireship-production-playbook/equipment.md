# Equipment: make one sensible purchase, then practise

**My recommendation:** test your current microphone first. If it is the weak link, choose a USB dynamic microphone, use existing wired headphones, keep your computer, and use your phone/webcam for occasional face segments. Jeff's exact current microphone and room are not verified here. This is a recommended kit for you, not a reproduction of his desk.

The [Shure announcement](https://www.shure.com/en-US/newsroom/shure-introduces-next-generation-of-the-mv6-usb-gaming-microphone) provides the dated price. Product pages establish specifications, not an independent sound-quality ranking. No sample from these microphones was recorded or compared for this course. Buy from an appropriate seller with return terms and check your computer's ports.

**Budget example:** $169 mic + $50 accessory allowance = $219 before tax/shipping, assuming existing headphones, computer and camera. You can begin at $169 with the included stand. A better room/position can be a more useful first change than any purchase. A dynamic capsule does not automatically remove background noise; distance, direction and environment matter.

The Audio-Technica ATR2100x-USB appears in many older recommendations, but its [current official US page](https://www.audio-technica.com/en-us/atr2100x-usb) says discontinued. I would not make it your default new purchase based on an old guide.

## Software: choose once

Default: [DaVinci Resolve free](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) + [OBS](https://obsproject.com/) + a simple graphics tool you already know. Resolve 21 is the researched live product baseline. Use the free edition's core tools; this course does not require Studio AI features.

If you already pay for Premiere, stay with it. [Premiere training](https://www.adobe.com/learn/premiere-pro) and [After Effects training](https://www.adobe.com/learn/after-effects) support a route closer to the documented historical tool choices. A paid subscription is not evidence your edit will improve. Current Adobe costs and entitlements depend on plan/region; inspect them before committing.

Do not buy a new camera, green screen, audio interface, high-end XLR mic, editing keyboard or effects subscription for the first practice video. Add equipment when a specific observed failure justifies it. Before changing computers, test a short 1080p project and proxy/preview options on what you have.
