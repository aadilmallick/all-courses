# Authorship and third-party material

The course explanations, dialogues, example sentences, learning activities, assessments, source summaries, and application source were authored for this request. Institutional references are linked, not redistributed as full books or scraped lessons. The source ledger reports inspected coverage and access limits. No native-speaker recordings, private family material, external photographs, or generated raster artwork are bundled.

The website uses Astro, Starlight, the Astro Markdown renderer, Sharp, and their transitive dependencies. Their exact versions and dependency integrity records are in `package-lock.json`. Development dependencies are not included in the archive; their notices and licenses accompany installation. Consult each package’s distributed license before redistributing a modified dependency bundle.

The inline architectural illustration is original SVG. System fonts are used. Browser speech synthesis, recognition and Chrome built-in AI are provided by the learner’s browser/device, subject to provider availability and terms. The course does not supply a voice license or claim synthetic audio is a native Hyderabadi recording.
