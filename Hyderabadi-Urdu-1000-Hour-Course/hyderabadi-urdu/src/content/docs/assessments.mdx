## The human part of each milestone

After chapter 2: a three-minute introduction and three follow-up questions. After chapter 4: a five-minute food, shopping or directions task with an unexpected change. After chapter 8: a two-minute story followed by eight minutes of questions. After chapter 12: a twenty-minute unscripted discussion plus narration, listening and mediation on fresh topics.

Score each dimension from 0 to 4: 0 cannot complete; 1 frequent breakdown; 2 completes with substantial help; 3 generally independent and understandable; 4 sustained, responsive and flexible. Dimensions: intelligibility, interaction, range, grammatical control, appropriate register, and repair. These are original learning rubrics, not an official CEFR validation. Aim for at least 3 in every dimension on two different occasions.

For listening, ask a partner for an unfamiliar two-minute family story at a natural but considerate pace. Hear it twice. Give the main point, four details, the speaker’s attitude where clear, and a fair summary. Transcribe a short excerpt in Roman script. Check with the speaker. Synthetic reading of a memorized exercise is not an unfamiliar listening assessment.
