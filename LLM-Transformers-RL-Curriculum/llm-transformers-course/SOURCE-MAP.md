All 18 supplied URLs were opened during research. This is a thematic prerequisite map, not a claim that every historical model name, leaderboard entry, or subsection in those very large primers has been reproduced. The course prioritizes derivations and transferable mechanisms. Production integrations and frontier empirical claims require their own implementation and evaluation.
