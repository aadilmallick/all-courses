# Provenance and third-party notices

The chapter explanations, synthetic examples, experiment implementations, and assembly code were authored for this curriculum. Course titles and links identify external sources; their content is not republished here. No full university lecture transcripts, videos, source textbooks, or private datasets are bundled.

MIT OpenCourseWare and Stanford materials retain their stated licenses and terms. Individual readings, data, code, and linked third-party materials can have different rights. A free-to-read link is not a blanket redistribution license. The resource inventory does not grant rights to linked content.

The offline reader bundles **Marked 17.0.5** and **KaTeX 0.16.22**, with license notices under `reader/vendor/`. KaTeX fonts retain their upstream notices. These are the only vendored runtime/build libraries. The laboratory itself uses browser APIs and original JavaScript. Python baseline examples use the standard library.

The authoring-only depth counter used **tiktoken 0.12.0**, `cl100k_base`. It is not bundled and is not needed to read the book or run examples. Its measured results are in `depth-report.json`. Runtime versions actually tested are recorded in `verification.md` and `outputs/runtime-versions.json`.
