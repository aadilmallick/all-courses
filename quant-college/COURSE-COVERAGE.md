# University course coverage and prerequisite map

This program teaches the major topic families below in original chapters and assigns the corresponding official lectures, notes, and available problems for full-course study. It does not reproduce source lectures or every university exercise. A graduate-level subject's representative derivation here is a bridge to its full notes, rather than a claim that all specialist proofs fit in one chapter. Read the listed prerequisites before opening the advanced source material.

## Added foundations and practical gaps

Algebra and trigonometry: OpenStax. Calculus: MIT 18.01SC and 18.02SC. Linear algebra: MIT 18.06SC. Probability: Harvard Stat 110. Programming: official Python and scientific-library documentation with original beginner instruction. Forecasting: authors' free FPP3 text. Practical additions include data provenance, point-in-time timing, survivorship, costs, microstructure, funding, capacity, leakage, research memos, and readiness assessment.

The companion implements fourteen selected experiments shared across chapters. It is not a complete numerical implementation of every advanced estimator, solver, or university assignment. The matrix and individual chapters explicitly identify source-based advanced work. Complete the original available problem sets and the studio projects to turn coverage into competence.
