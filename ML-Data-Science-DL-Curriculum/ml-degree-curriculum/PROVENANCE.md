# Material provenance

This integrated course was created for the current ML and data-science request. Its classical-ML chapters, prerequisite bridges, source map, classical model implementations, advanced finite experiments, and project protocol were authored for this package.

Relevant mathematical, transformer, RL, and PyTorch material was adapted from the user's previously generated `LLM-Transformers-RL-Curriculum.zip` and `PyTorch-Complete-Curriculum.zip`. Their original independent scalar sensitivity appendices were omitted from integrated chapters; the retained examples are tied to the actual chapter topics. Navigation, paths, dependencies, and validation were integrated into this course.

The seven PyTorch workshops are included as longer companion treatments. Selected workshop sections also appear inside core lessons. This intentional overlap supports a self-contained reading path but means a total word count does not measure unique instructional prose. Old validation reports were not copied as evidence for this run.

The course itself does not imply a transfer of rights in third-party references. External source material is linked and summarized rather than included wholesale.
