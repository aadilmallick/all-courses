# Make progress. Show the work.

A practical guide to starting a self-improvement YouTube channel, prepared for Aadil on 13 November 2026.

**Open [guide.html](guide.html)** for the complete illustrated reader. Start with [start-today.html](start-today.html) when you want to record immediately. The [two production checklists](production-playbooks.html) are designed to stay beside your editor.

The plan assumes **8–10 hours a week**, a phone you already own, and a new practice of speaking to camera. It recommends one channel for ambitious learners and early-career professionals, with two formats: direct advice and occasional polished experiments. That positioning is a hypothesis to validate with real viewers.

Useful extras: [90 daily actions](daily-actions.md), [original scripts](sample-scripts.md), [templates](templates.md), [equipment decisions](equipment.md), [resources and research limits](sources.md), and [verification](verification.md).

No paid course or new camera is required. All Markdown files can be read without installing software. The HTML reader works offline; external learning links require internet access. To regenerate it, use Node 18+ and run `node build.mjs` from this folder. Marked 17.0.5 is bundled with its MIT license. The guide is the deliverable; there is no account integration, auto-publishing, tracking, or revenue prediction.

The creator analysis distinguishes visible evidence, public process descriptions, and our original adaptations. It does not claim access to either creator’s private analytics or current production team. Full transcripts, screenshots, and source videos are not redistributed.
