# Authoring brief

- Audience: Aadil, a software engineer and ambitious learner, beginning a self-improvement channel.
- Constraint: 8–10 hours weekly. Prior preference for screen recording does not establish comfort on camera; delivery practice is included.
- Requested outcome: a complete practical guide, two creator-inspired production methods, linked learning resources, growth principles, and daily actions. This is a focused guide rather than a new comprehensive textbook or a promise to reproduce a creator’s private workflow.
- Scope: authentic experience-led advice, modest personal experiments, filming, editing, packaging, analytics, consistency, and a small relevant audience-to-product path.
- Boundaries: no invented transformation, private analytics, exact current gear claims, diagnosis, medical protocol, or guaranteed growth/income.
- Deliverables: eight instructional chapters, assembled HTML/Markdown, worked scripts, templates, equipment guidance, two production checklists, daily actions, provenance, verification, and portable ZIP.
- Evidence: primary creator pages, direct inspection of one public video from each creator, official YouTube documentation, and official equipment/editor resources. Access detail is in sources.md.
- Conventions: minutes and US dollars; equipment allowances are estimates unless explicitly identified as a checked price. Day numbers are relative to starting, not scheduled dates. R = direct advice, P = polished teaching/experiment.
