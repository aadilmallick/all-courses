# 08. Hints

1. Introduce $u=x-ct$ and differentiate any twice-differentiable function of $u$ first. Evaluate its time derivative at zero.
2. Use $c=\sqrt{\mathcal T/\mu}$, $f_1=c/(2L)$, and $\lambda_n=2L/n$.
3. For different indices, both resulting cosine integrals have nonzero integer wave numbers. For matching indices, one cosine becomes the constant one.
4. Each spatial mode keeps its shape while its amplitude oscillates at its own frequency. Substitute the specified position and time only after writing the motion.
5. Power transmission is not simply the square of displacement transmission; include the impedance ratio.
6. Average power scales with $A^2\omega^2$ for the same string.
7. Differentiate the dispersion relation. Compare its slope with the slope of a line from the origin to the point.
8. Source motion changes emitted wavelength; observer motion changes the rate of crossing crests in a fixed emitted pattern.
