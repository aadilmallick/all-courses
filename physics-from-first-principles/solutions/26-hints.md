# 26. Hints

1. Substitute $u=bx$ and account for the orientation of integration limits.
2. The left branch is proportional to $x$, the right to $L-x$; integrate across the source.
3. For a delta source, convolution selects the Green function itself.
4. Integrate $-\partial^\mu\phi\partial_\mu\eta$ by parts.
5. The off-diagonal entry is multiplied by $1-2p$; compute $\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2$ directly.
6. Differentiate $\ln Z$ at fixed gap, not at fixed $\beta\Delta$.
7. Substitute the stationary-point relation $bm^2=a(T_c-T)$ after differentiating.
8. The two terms in the free-energy integrand must have the same units; without a quartic term a negative quadratic coefficient is unstable.
