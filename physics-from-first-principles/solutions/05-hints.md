# 05. Hints

1. Integrate the force before applying the work theorem. A force increasing linearly from zero does not do $F_{\rm final}\Delta x$ work.
2. Initial spring energy is positive. Subtract the magnitude of the resistance work, then solve for speed.
3. Treat the sticking and elastic cases as different physical models. Momentum is conserved in both; kinetic energy only in the elastic case.
4. Use the decomposition into center-of-mass and relative kinetic energies. The relative velocity is zero after sticking.
5. Use a dimensionless mass ratio inside the logarithm. Compare $\ln(2R)$ with $2\ln R$.
6. Circular force balance gives speed; escape is a separate zero-energy threshold. Use circumference divided by circular speed for period.
7. Differentiate $U$. At each root, inspect the second derivative or the local shape.
8. The surface cannot pull the particle into contact. Set the support force nonnegative after writing the inward radial force equation.
