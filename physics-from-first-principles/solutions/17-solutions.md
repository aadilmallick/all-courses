# 17. Solutions

1. Currents are $9/100=0.090\,\mathrm A$ and $9/200=0.045\,\mathrm A$. Total is $0.135\,\mathrm A$, so equivalent resistance is $9/0.135\approx66.7\,\Omega$. Power is $9(0.135)=1.215\,\mathrm W$, matching the sum of branch powers.
2. $RC=(0.50\times10^6)(2.0\times10^{-6})=1.0\,\mathrm s$. After 2.0 seconds the fraction is $1-e^{-2}\approx0.865$.
3. $\hat{\mathbf x}\times\hat{\mathbf z}=-\hat{\mathbf y}$. A positive charge feels force toward $-y$; a negative charge toward $+y$. The force does not change speed in a magnetic field alone.
4. $B=(\mu_0/2\pi)(5/0.050)\approx2.0\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm T$. Direction circles the wire by the current right-hand rule.
5. Emf magnitude is $A|dB/dt|=0.0060\,\mathrm V$. The induced field should be downward, so current is clockwise viewed from above. This opposes the increase in upward flux.
6. Stored energy is $0.5(0.20)(3.0)^2=0.90\,\mathrm J$. Rise rate is $30\,\mathrm{A\,s^{-1}}$, so voltage magnitude is $0.20(30)=6.0\,\mathrm V$.
7. $LQ''+Q/C=0$ gives $\omega_0^2=1/(LC)$. With the given values, $LC=10^{-6}\,\mathrm{s^2}$, so $\omega_0=1000\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$ and frequency is approximately 159 Hz.
8. $\widetilde I=-i\omega C\widetilde V$, hence $Z_C=\widetilde V/\widetilde I=1/(-i\omega C)=i/(\omega C)$. The complex phase sign depends on the convention used to reconstruct real time signals. Translating the entire convention gives the same physical phase relationship and measured result.
