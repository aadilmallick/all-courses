# 14. Hints

1. Count possible positions of the one head; use a complement for at least one.
2. Compute $\langle X\rangle$ and $\langle X^2\rangle$ separately.
3. A Poisson variance equals its mean.
4. Include the excited-state degeneracy in the partition sum.
5. Differentiate each exponential term and divide by $Z$.
6. The rms speed scales as the inverse square root of mass.
7. Substitute $e^{\beta(\epsilon-\mu)}=2$.
8. Expand $e^x$ near $x=0$; a harmonic oscillator has both kinetic and potential quadratic terms.
