# Chapter 02 worked solutions

1. $135^\circ=3\pi/4$ rad. The unit-circle point is in quadrant two, so cosine is $-1/\sqrt2$ and sine is $+1/\sqrt2$.
2. B minus A is $(3,4,12)$ m. Its squared magnitude is $9+16+144=169$ m², giving separation 13 m. The displacement is the full vector, while the separation is its nonnegative magnitude.
3. Components are $(20\cos60^\circ,20\sin60^\circ)=(10,10\sqrt3)$ N, approximately $(10,17.32)$ N. Squaring and adding gives $100+300=400$ N², consistent with magnitude 20 N.
4. The sum is $(2,3,3)$ m. Its magnitude is $\sqrt{4+9+9}=\sqrt{22}$ m. Its unit direction is $(2,3,3)/\sqrt{22}$, which is dimensionless and has norm one.
5. The dot product is $1(2)+2(-1)=0$. Both inputs are nonzero, so they are perpendicular. Their cross product is $(0,0,1(-1)-2(2))=(0,0,-5)$. The negative $z$ direction follows the order from $\mathbf a$ to $\mathbf b$. Each magnitude is $\sqrt5$, so the area magnitude is 5, independently checking the result.
6. Land velocity is $(2,3)$ m/s. Its speed is $\sqrt{13}\approx3.606$ m/s and displacement after 20 s is $(40,60)$ m under the constant-current model. The direction is approximately $56.31^\circ$ north of east.
7. With $\phi=\pi/4$, $a_{x'}=2/\sqrt2=\sqrt2$ m and $a_{y'}=-\sqrt2$ m. The squared magnitude remains $2+2=4$ m². The physical vector has not rotated.
8. Expand $|\mathbf a+\mathbf b|^2=|\mathbf a|^2+|\mathbf b|^2+2|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\theta$. Since cosine is at most one, this is at most $(|\mathbf a|+|\mathbf b|)^2$. Both sides to be compared are nonnegative, so taking square roots preserves the inequality. For nonzero inputs equality requires $\cos\theta=1$, meaning the same direction. If either input is zero, equality also holds.
