# 16. Hints

1. Laplace's equation permits constants and linear functions.
2. The image has charge $-q$ at distance $2a$ from the real charge.
3. Differentiate in $x$ and $y$ separately and remember $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$.
4. The monopole terms cancel; retain the first moment.
5. Isolated means charge stays fixed, not voltage.
6. Fixed voltage means $Q=CV$ increases with $C$.
7. Uniform polarization has no divergence; surface normals set the signs.
8. A squared gradient cannot be negative.
