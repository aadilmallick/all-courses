# Chapter 27 hints

1. Expand both cubes before subtracting; the even powers of the increment cancel.
2. Use endpoint half-weights and compare with the exact value $1/3$.
3. Solve $2^{-n}\le10^{-6}$ for integer $n$.
4. Stability uses absolute multiplier below one; nonnegativity requires multiplier at least zero.
5. Square the two Euler updates and add them.
6. Isolate $\Delta t$ from $D\Delta t/(\Delta x)^2\le1/2$.
7. Standard error scales inversely with square root of sample count.
8. Decide whether each check concerns the equation or the real apparatus.
