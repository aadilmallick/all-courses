# 25. Worked solutions

1. $dx=\cos\theta dr-r\sin\theta d\theta$ and $dy=\sin\theta dr+r\cos\theta d\theta$. Squaring and adding gives $dr^2+r^2d\theta^2$. The inverse metric has entries $1,r^{-2}$. The Christoffel formula gives $\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta}=-r$ and $\Gamma^\theta{}_{r\theta}=\Gamma^\theta{}_{\theta r}=1/r$.
2. $R^r{}_{\theta r\theta}=\partial_r\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\theta}-\partial_\theta\Gamma^r{}_{r\theta}+\Gamma^r{}_{r\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda{}_{\theta\theta}-\Gamma^r{}_{\theta\lambda}\Gamma^\lambda{}_{r\theta}=-1+0+0-(-r)(1/r)=0$. The result is flatness, despite nonzero connection coefficients.
3. $gh/c^2\approx1.09\times10^{-14}$. Over one day the higher stationary clock gains approximately $9.43\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm s=0.943\,\mathrm{ns}$ relative to the lower one, neglecting motion and field variation.
4. The radius is about $29.5\,\mathrm{km}$. The invariant $48G^2M^2/(c^4r^6)$ is finite at $r=r_s>0$; the Schwarzschild-coordinate divergence there is removable by another chart. This statement does not make the curvature zero.
5. $\nu_\infty/\nu_e=\sqrt{1-r_s/(4r_s)}=\sqrt3/2\approx0.8660$. The photon is redshifted for these stationary observers.
6. The continuity equation gives $\rho\propto a^{-4}$. In a flat radiation-only model with no cosmological constant, $H^2\propto a^{-4}$, so $\dot a\propto a^{-1}$ and $a^2\propto t$, hence $a\propto t^{1/2}$.
7. $1+z=2$, so $z=1$. Photon energy falls by a factor of two. This statement concerns free propagation in the chosen homogeneous background.
8. For $a\propto t^{2/3}$, $H=\dot a/a=2/(3t)$. At the present time, $t_0=2/(3H_0)$. Adding vacuum energy changes $H(a)$ and the integral of $dt=da/[aH(a)]$, so the age relation changes.
