# Chapter 01 hints

1. Multiply by 1,000 m/km and divide by 3,600 s/h.
2. Convert each side before multiplying, or square the centimetre conversion.
3. Subtract final minus initial coordinate. A path may include reversals.
4. First subtract $D_0$ from both sides. Do not divide by zero.
5. A cubic centimetre is $10^{-6}$ m³.
6. Find the dimension of $m/k$ before taking the root.
7. Apply each percentage to its own starting value.
8. Slope is position change divided by elapsed time; use either point for the intercept.
