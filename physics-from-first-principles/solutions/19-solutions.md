# 19. Solutions

1. Norm squared is $1+3=4$, so normalized vector is $(1/2,i\sqrt3/2)^T$. Probabilities are 1/4 and 3/4.
2. Amplitude is $(1+e^{i\phi})/2$, so $P(+)=[2+2\cos\phi]/4=(1+\cos\phi)/2$. Values are 1,1/2,0 at the three stated phases. Relative phase changes a measurable probability.
3. $XZ=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}=-iY$ and $ZX=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}=iY$. Thus $[X,Z]=-2iY$.
4. The characteristic equation is $(E_0-E)^2-g^2=0$. Eigenvalues are $E_0+g$ with vector $(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$, and $E_0-g$ with vector $(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$. If $g=0$, both eigenvalues coincide and every normalized vector in this two-dimensional space is an energy eigenvector; one may choose any orthonormal basis.
5. In state 0, $\langle X\rangle=0$ and $\langle X^2\rangle=1$, giving $\Delta X=1$. For $Z$, $\langle Z\rangle=1$ and $\Delta Z=0$. A single $X$ measurement gives±1, never 0; a $Z$ measurement gives+1 with certainty.
6. $U^\dagger=I\cos\theta+iX\sin\theta$ since $X$ is Hermitian. Product is $I\cos^2\theta+X^2\sin^2\theta$ because cross terms cancel. With $X^2=I$, it equals identity.
7. First complete transfer is $t=\pi/\Omega$. At $\pi/(2\Omega)$ the probability is $\sin^2(\pi/4)=1/2$. At $2\pi/\Omega$ the state is $-|0\rangle$; every projective probability is unchanged by multiplication of the entire state by−1. Relative phases with another coherent branch would require describing that larger complete state.
8. Maximum probability is $\Omega^2/(4\Omega^2)=1/4$, and effective angular frequency in the sine argument's underlying two-level splitting is $\sqrt{\Omega^2+\delta^2}=2\Omega$. For $\delta=0$, the prefactor becomes 1 and the formula reduces to $\sin^2(\Omega t/2)$.
