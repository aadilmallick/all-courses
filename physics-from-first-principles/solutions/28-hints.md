# Chapter 28 hints

1. Use $n-1$ for sample variance and $s/\sqrt n$ for standard error under independence.
2. Integrate $x^2/q$ over the symmetric interval $[-q/2,q/2]$.
3. Differentiate with respect to $L$ and $T$ separately.
4. Write both readings using the same offset or scale symbol.
5. Compute means, centred sums, fit coefficients and residuals in that order.
6. Apply the derivative of $T^2$ to a small time error.
7. Count the remaining degrees of freedom and ask what predicts new observations.
8. State a question that could yield a useful bound even if no effect is resolved.
