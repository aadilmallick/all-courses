2. The energy formula has (L^{-2}); the center coordinate has (L).
3. Use the antiderivative $x/L-\sin(2\pi x/L)/(2\pi)$.
4. First find (B/A) and (C/A); transmission includes (k_2/k_1).
5. At (a), match the value and slope of $A\sin kx$.
6. Divide the two exponentials; do not recompute their prefactors.
7. Differentiate the Gaussian, or use the Gaussian second moment. For momentum use the second derivative in the kinetic operator.
8. Keep both cross terms when multiplying $\psi^*\psi$; orthogonality removes their integrals, not their pointwise values.
