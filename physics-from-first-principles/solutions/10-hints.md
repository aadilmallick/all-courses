# 10. Hints

1. The recorded moving-clock interval is proper time. Multiply by $\gamma$ for laboratory time before multiplying by speed for laboratory distance.
2. The laboratory procedure uses simultaneous endpoint events in the laboratory frame.
3. Compare $c\Delta t$ with $\Delta x$. Set $\Delta t'=0$ in the Lorentz transformation for the second part.
4. Use the denominator in the velocity-addition rule. Ordinary addition is only a low-speed approximation.
5. Use the receding longitudinal Doppler factor and keep the source's proper frequency distinct from a coordinate emission frequency.
6. Begin with $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$, then use $u=pc^2/E$.
7. Initial momentum is zero. Total energy becomes rest energy of the stationary composite if none escapes.
8. Substitute $q=Ft/(mc)$ in the integral. Expand $\sqrt{1+q^2}$ for small $q$.
