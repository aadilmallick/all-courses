# 15. Hints

1. Calculate magnitude with $k_e|Q|/r^2$ and use the negative source sign for direction.
2. Horizontal components cancel; divide the remaining component by the source distance once more for projection.
3. Differentiate the potential first, then apply the component definitions.
4. Use enclosed charge $Q(r/R)^3$ inside.
5. Opposite faces have opposite outward normals.
6. Calculate derivatives one component at a time.
7. Fields from the two sheets add between them; then use $|\Delta V|=Ed$.
8. The field is singular at a point that lies inside the integration region.
