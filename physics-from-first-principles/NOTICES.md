# Provenance and bundled software

The explanatory course text, examples, diagrams and companion are original material generated for this request. They are an unofficial learning companion and are not endorsed by Caltech, the Feynman estate, MIT, NIST or BIPM. Source citations identify conceptual references, coverage benchmarks and constants, not ownership of the original teaching text.

The user-supplied Feynman PDF and full extracted text are excluded. The official Caltech online edition is linked for optional reading. Its availability to read does not grant redistribution rights; consult its own terms for use of source prose, images and recordings.

The offline reader bundles KaTeX 0.16.22 (MIT License) and marked 17.0.5 (MIT License). The marked version was verified from its distribution header; the KaTeX version is checked from its exported version. Both are listed in dependencies.json. The original license files accompany them in companion/vendor/. No third-party book figures or passages have been copied into the course.

The scientific models use standard mathematics and stated approximations. JavaScript sources have no runtime package dependencies. The authoring token counter and browser test driver are not runtime requirements and their installed dependency directories are excluded from the ZIP.
